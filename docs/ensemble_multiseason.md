# NHC-GEFS CCSR across seasons, 2018–2026

CCSH = CCSR with the centre swapped for HCCA (NHC's early corrected consensus, public in real time); same members and predicted spread, k refit leaving the season out. GECO = CCSH without the 12-h lead smoothing of the centre (HCCA used as issued); the chosen GEFS product (src/tcic/ensemble/geco.py).

Fair CRPS (kt; point forecasts scored as |error|) on identical cases: every NHC synoptic cycle with NHC's GEFS member tracks (newest run ≥ 6 h old), HCCA, IVCN and OFCL all present; “dressed” = the point forecast as a Gaussian with its own per-lead RMS error from the other 2018–24 seasons (the fair probabilistic baseline: an ensemble always beats its own point forecast in CRPS); leads 12–120 h every 12 h; best track (2026: working best track). 2018–24 are fully out of sample (consensus centre, spread model and k all leave-one-season-out); 2025–26 use the production models. GEFS v11 (20 members) until 2020-09, v12 (30) after. `*` = 95% storm-block bootstrap CI excludes 0.
